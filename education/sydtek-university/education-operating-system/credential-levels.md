# Credential Levels

## Purpose

Defines how SydTek University distinguishes certificates, undergraduate programs, graduate programs, and doctoral programs.

## Certificate Level

A certificate proves focused competency.

### Evidence

- Skills practice
- Applied assessment
- Final demonstration

### Standard

The learner can perform a defined skill.

## Undergraduate Level

An undergraduate program proves broad foundational mastery.

### Evidence

- General education
- Major coursework
- Applied projects
- Portfolio
- Capstone

### Standard

The learner understands the field and can apply its foundations.

## Graduate Level

A graduate program proves advanced professional or scholarly mastery.

### Evidence

- Advanced coursework
- Research or applied project
- Case studies
- Practicum
- Thesis or capstone

### Standard

The learner can analyze, design, lead, and solve complex problems.

## Doctoral Level

A doctoral program proves original contribution.

### Evidence

- Research agenda
- Literature review
- Methodology
- Dissertation or doctoral project
- Public defense
- Contribution to knowledge

### Standard

The learner contributes something new, defensible, and documented.

## Core Principle

Each level must require stronger proof than the level before it.
