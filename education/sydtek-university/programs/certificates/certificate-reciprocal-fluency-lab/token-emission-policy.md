# Token Emission Policy

## Purpose

This document defines how token-based recognition may be issued without weakening the certificate program.

## Primary Rule

Token emissions must never exceed verified value creation.

## Emission Philosophy

Tokens are not used to bribe participation.

Tokens are used to recognize completed proof, service, mentorship, and contribution.

## Approved Emission Categories

### Certification Completion

Limited recognition after final assessment is passed.

### Mentor Service

Recognition for helping another student complete certification.

### Translation Contribution

Recognition for approved bilingual content, curriculum, or outreach work.

### Scholarship Support

Tokens may support access for students who cannot afford advanced certification.

### Governance Participation

Only verified contributors may participate in program governance.

## Emission Controls

Token issuance must be:

- Capped
- Scheduled
- Reviewed
- Logged
- Approved
- Tied to proof

## No Open Faucet

The program must never operate as an open reward faucet.

## Suggested Emission Rule

No reward may be issued unless at least one of the following is verified:

- Student passed a final assessment
- Student served as approved mentor
- Student completed approved translation work
- Student supported program operations
- Student contributed to verified curriculum expansion

## Vesting

Rewards may be subject to vesting.

Suggested vesting:

- 25% immediately after approval
- 25% after 30 days of continued good standing
- 25% after 60 days
- 25% after 90 days

## Revocation

Rewards may be revoked or frozen if fraud is discovered before full vesting.

## Core Principle

Rewards must follow verified value.
