# Final Certification Process

## Purpose

To verify that the student has achieved measurable conversational fluency.

## Certification Requirement

Students must complete:

- Required sessions
- All logs and reflections
- Recorded fluency samples
- Final oral assessment

## Final Assessment Format

### Part 1 — Introduction

Student introduces themselves in target language.

### Part 2 — Guided Conversation

Instructor asks structured questions.

Topics may include:

- Family
- Work / school
- Daily life
- Faith / values
- Culture

### Part 3 — Live Response

Student responds to unexpected prompts.

### Part 4 — Listening Comprehension

Student listens and responds to spoken phrases.

### Part 5 — Correction Application

Student receives correction and repeats correctly.

## Scoring

Based on rubric:

- Speaking
- Listening
- Pronunciation
- Response speed
- Correction application

## Certification Levels

### Level 1 — Basic Fluency

- Can hold simple conversation
- Understands common phrases

### Level 2 — Functional Fluency

- Can navigate real-life situations
- Responds with moderate confidence

### Level 3 — Applied Fluency

- Maintains conversation with ease
- Handles varied topics

### Level 4 — Advanced Fluency

- Speaks naturally with minimal hesitation
- Demonstrates cultural awareness

## Certification Output

Students receive:

- Certificate (PDF / digital)
- Fluency level designation
- Instructor evaluation summary
- Recorded assessment archive

## Core Principle

Fluency must be demonstrated live.

Not claimed.

Not assumed.

Proven.
