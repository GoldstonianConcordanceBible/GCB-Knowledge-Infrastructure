# Workforce Alignment

## Purpose

This document defines how the Fluency Lab connects language certification to real-world opportunity.

## Core Claim

Bilingual fluency has workforce value.

The certificate becomes stronger when employers, ministries, schools, and service organizations recognize its usefulness.

## Workforce Use Cases

### Customer Service

Students demonstrate ability to support bilingual customers.

### Healthcare Support

Students gain practical communication skills for non-clinical support environments.

### Trade School Support

Students learn language needed for job sites, tools, safety, scheduling, and customer communication.

### Ministry and Missions

Students practice language for prayer, service, teaching, and community outreach.

### Education

Students support bilingual tutoring, classroom help, and parent communication.

### Small Business

Students improve communication with customers, vendors, and local communities.

## Skill Evidence

Employers or partners may review:

- Certificate level
- Instructor evaluation
- Speaking sample
- Reputation score
- Service record

## Workforce Levels

### Basic

Can greet, introduce, and answer simple questions.

### Functional

Can assist in common service situations.

### Applied

Can hold practical workplace conversation.

### Advanced

Can support bilingual communication with confidence.

## Core Principle

The certificate should connect to opportunity beyond the classroom.
