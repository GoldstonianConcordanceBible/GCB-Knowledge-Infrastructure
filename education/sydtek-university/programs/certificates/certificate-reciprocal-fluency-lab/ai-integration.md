# AI Integration Layer

## Purpose

Defines how AI may assist the Fluency Lab without replacing human conversation.

## Core Rule

AI supports fluency.

AI does not replace human interaction.

## Approved AI Roles

### Pronunciation Feedback

AI may analyze speech clarity and suggest corrections.

### Vocabulary Support

AI may suggest words during or after sessions.

### Session Review

AI may summarize conversation recordings.

### Fraud Detection

AI may detect:

- Repeated scripts
- Identical responses
- Non-human patterns
- Audio manipulation

### Progress Tracking

AI may track improvement trends over time.

## Prohibited AI Roles

AI must not:

- Replace partner conversation
- Complete assessments for students
- Generate fake session logs
- Certify students
- Override instructor judgment

## Future Expansion

- Speech-to-text scoring
- Accent clarity metrics
- Real-time correction assistant
- Conversation difficulty scaling

## Core Principle

AI enhances practice.

Humans prove fluency.
