# Proof-of-Fluency Network

## Purpose

The Proof-of-Fluency Network is the reputation and verification layer for the SydTek Fluency Lab.

It records who has practiced, who has improved, who has certified, and who is trusted to help others.

## Core Idea

Fluency must be proven through live interaction.

## Network Participants

### Learners

Students enrolled in the certificate program.

### Native Speaker Partners

Students or participants who help another learner practice.

### Peer Mentors

Certified learners approved to support new students.

### Instructors

Program evaluators who review logs, recordings, and final assessments.

### Certifiers

Advanced evaluators approved to issue final certification decisions.

## Proof Types

The network may recognize:

- Proof of Session
- Proof of Correction
- Proof of Speaking
- Proof of Listening
- Proof of Improvement
- Proof of Service
- Proof of Certification

## Verification Sources

Proof may come from:

- Partner verification
- Instructor review
- Recorded speaking samples
- Assessment rubric scores
- Reflection journals
- Final oral exam
- Mentor reports

## Reputation Scores

Students may receive internal reputation scores for:

- Reliability
- Speaking growth
- Listening growth
- Correction quality
- Partner support
- Cultural respect
- Mentor readiness

## Core Principle

The network does not reward noise.

The network rewards verified growth.
