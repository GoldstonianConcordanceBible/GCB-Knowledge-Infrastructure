# Scholarship Model

## Purpose

This document defines how scholarships may support access to the Fluency Lab.

## Why Scholarships Matter

A fluency certification program should not only serve students who can pay.

Scholarships allow the program to support families, homeschoolers, ministry learners, workforce learners, and underserved communities.

## Scholarship Sources

Scholarships may be funded through:

- Donors
- Sponsors
- Institutional funds
- DAO-approved support
- Token-based scholarship pools
- Workforce partners

## Scholarship Eligibility

Students may qualify based on:

- Financial need
- Ministry service
- Workforce development goals
- Academic goals
- Community impact
- Mentor recommendation

## Scholarship Requirements

Scholarship students must:

- Attend sessions
- Complete logs
- Participate respectfully
- Submit assessments
- Remain in good standing

## Scholarship Protection

Scholarship funds should support learning, not farming.

Scholarship support may be paused if:

- Student stops attending
- Student submits false logs
- Student violates conduct rules
- Student avoids assessment

## Core Principle

Access should expand without weakening standards.
