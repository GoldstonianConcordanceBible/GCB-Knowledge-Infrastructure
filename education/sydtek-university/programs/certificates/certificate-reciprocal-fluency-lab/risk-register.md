# Risk Register

## Purpose

Identifies risks and mitigation strategies.

## Risk 1 — Low-Quality Participation

Mitigation:

- Instructor review
- Session audits
- Final assessment requirement

## Risk 2 — Reward Farming

Mitigation:

- Token guardrails
- Anti-farming controls
- Delayed rewards
- Reputation gating

## Risk 3 — Partner Mismatch

Mitigation:

- Re-matching system
- Instructor oversight
- Level-based pairing

## Risk 4 — Drop-Off

Mitigation:

- Weekly structure
- Partner accountability
- Progress tracking

## Risk 5 — Weak Certification

Mitigation:

- Strict rubric
- Live oral exam
- Instructor authority

## Risk 6 — Over-Reliance on Token

Mitigation:

- Certificate-first design
- Limited emissions
- Governance controls

## Risk 7 — Scaling Too Fast

Mitigation:

- Pilot first
- Controlled growth
- Mentor layer before expansion

## Core Principle

Protect the system before scaling the system.
