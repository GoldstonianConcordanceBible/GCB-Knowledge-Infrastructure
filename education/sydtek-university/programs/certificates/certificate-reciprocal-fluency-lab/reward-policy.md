# Reward Policy

## Purpose

This document defines how rewards may be issued inside the Learned & Earned Fluency Certification Model.

## Core Rule

Rewards are never payment for attendance.

Rewards are recognition for demonstrated fluency, service, and verified contribution.

## Reward Hierarchy

### 1. Certificate

The certificate is the primary reward.

### 2. Fluency Level

The student receives a verified fluency designation.

### 3. Reputation

The student earns status inside the Proof-of-Fluency Network.

### 4. Opportunity

The student may qualify for mentoring, tutoring, workforce, ministry, or advanced study opportunities.

### 5. Token Recognition

Token-based recognition may be issued only when approved by program governance.

## Approved Reward Events

Rewards may be issued for:

- Passing final fluency assessment
- Advancing to a higher fluency level
- Becoming a verified peer mentor
- Completing instructor-approved service hours
- Helping another learner reach certification
- Contributing approved curriculum resources
- Supporting translation or bilingual outreach work

## Prohibited Reward Events

Rewards may not be issued for:

- Logging in
- Watching videos
- Joining a server
- Submitting unverified logs
- Attending silently
- Repeating fake sessions
- Creating multiple accounts
- Recruiting low-quality users

## Reward Timing

Rewards are issued after verification.

No reward is issued before assessment.

## Anti-Farming Rule

Any student suspected of farming rewards may have rewards paused, reviewed, reduced, or revoked.

## Core Principle

Earned means proven.
