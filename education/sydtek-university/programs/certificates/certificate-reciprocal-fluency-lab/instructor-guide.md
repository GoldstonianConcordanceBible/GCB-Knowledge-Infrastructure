# Instructor Guide

## Role of Instructor

The instructor is not the primary speaker.

The instructor is:

- Observer
- Evaluator
- Corrector
- Standard keeper

## Responsibilities

- Review session logs
- Monitor student engagement
- Evaluate recorded conversations
- Provide correction guidance
- Conduct final assessments
- Approve certification

## Intervention Points

Instructor steps in when:

- Students are not speaking target language
- Corrections are not being applied
- One partner dominates conversation
- Progress stalls

## Feedback Model

Feedback should be:

- Clear
- Specific
- Actionable
- Encouraging but honest

## Evaluation Frequency

- Weekly review (light)
- Midpoint evaluation
- Final assessment

## Instructor Tools

- Session logs
- Audio/video recordings
- Rubric scoring sheet
- Progress tracker

## Core Principle

Do not carry the conversation.

Force the student to speak.
