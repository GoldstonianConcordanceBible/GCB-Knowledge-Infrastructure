# Weekly Schedule

## Standard 8-Week Certificate

### Week 1 — Orientation and Baseline

- Program orientation
- Partner matching
- Baseline speaking sample
- First conversation log

### Week 2 — Introductions and Family

- Basic introductions
- Family vocabulary
- Partner correction round
- Reflection journal

### Week 3 — Daily Life

- Morning and evening routines
- Food and home conversation
- Pronunciation review

### Week 4 — School, Work, and Service

- Practical vocabulary
- Role-play
- Midpoint instructor review

### Week 5 — Culture and Community

- Cultural conversation
- Listening comprehension
- Partner feedback

### Week 6 — Problem Solving

- Asking for help
- Clarifying meaning
- Recovering from mistakes

### Week 7 — Fluency Pressure

- Timed conversation
- Unexpected prompts
- Correction application

### Week 8 — Final Certification

- Final oral assessment
- Instructor evaluation
- Certificate decision
- Student reflection
