# Partner Matching System

## Purpose

The Partner Matching System ensures that every learner is paired with a native speaker who can support real fluency development.

This is the engine of the Fluency Lab.

## Matching Criteria

Students are matched based on:

- Native language
- Target language
- Skill level (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced)
- Availability (time zones, schedule)
- Age group (youth, college, adult)
- Program type (homeschool, workforce, ministry, academic)

## Pair Types

### Standard Pair

1 English Speaker  
1 Spanish Speaker  

Each teaches the other.

### Guided Pair

2 students + 1 instructor

Used for:
- Beginners
- Younger students
- Structured classroom settings

### Group Pod (Advanced)

2–4 students rotating roles

Used for:
- Debate
- Group conversation
- Cultural discussion

## Matching Process

1. Student registers
2. Language level assessed
3. Profile created
4. Matching algorithm or manual pairing
5. Intro session scheduled
6. Partner agreement confirmed

## Partner Agreement

Each student agrees to:

- Show up consistently
- Speak in target language during sessions
- Provide respectful correction
- Complete session logs
- Participate in assessments

## Re-Matching Policy

Students may request reassignment if:

- Schedule conflict
- Communication breakdown
- Skill mismatch
- Inactivity

## Instructor Oversight

Instructors monitor:

- Session logs
- Recorded conversations
- Partner feedback
- Progress trends

## Core Principle

Your partner is your classroom.

Your conversation is your curriculum.
