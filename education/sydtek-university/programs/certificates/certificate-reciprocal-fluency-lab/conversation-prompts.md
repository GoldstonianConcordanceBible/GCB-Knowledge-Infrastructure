# Conversation Prompts

## Level 1 — Basic

### Introductions

- What is your name?
- Where are you from?
- How old are you?
- What language are you learning?
- Why do you want to learn this language?

### Family

- Who is in your family?
- Do you have brothers or sisters?
- What is your family like?
- What do you do with your family?

### Daily Life

- What time do you wake up?
- What do you eat for breakfast?
- What do you do during the day?
- What do you do at night?

## Level 2 — Functional

### School and Work

- What do you study?
- What kind of work do you do?
- What is difficult about your day?
- What do you enjoy learning?

### Food

- What food do you like?
- What food do you cook?
- What food is common in your culture?
- What meal reminds you of home?

### Travel

- Where have you traveled?
- Where would you like to go?
- How do you ask for directions?
- What do you need at an airport?

## Level 3 — Applied

### Culture

- What is important in your culture?
- How do families celebrate?
- What traditions do you value?
- What is often misunderstood about your culture?

### Faith and Values

- What values guide your life?
- How do you define service?
- What does community mean to you?
- How can language help people understand each other?

## Level 4 — Advanced

### Debate and Reflection

- Should every student learn a second language?
- How does language shape identity?
- What is lost when a language disappears?
- How does bilingualism help families, churches, schools, and businesses?
