# Accreditation Notes

## Institutional Purpose

The Reciprocal Fluency Lab supports credit-bearing language learning through documented practice, measurable outcomes, and formal assessment.

## Evidence Package

For institutional review, maintain:

- Program overview
- Learning outcomes
- Credit hour structure
- Assessment rubric
- Instructor guide
- Session logs
- Final certification records
- Student reflection samples
- Recorded oral assessments

## Academic Justification

This program qualifies as a practicum because students complete supervised applied learning through real-world conversation.

## Key Claim

The certificate does not award credit for exposure.

It awards credit for demonstrated fluency.

## Review Standard

A reviewer should be able to verify:

- What the student practiced
- How often the student practiced
- Who verified the practice
- What outcomes were assessed
- How fluency was measured
- Why the certificate was awarded
