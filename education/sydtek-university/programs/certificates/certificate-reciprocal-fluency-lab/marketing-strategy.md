# Marketing Strategy

## Purpose

Defines how the Fluency Lab is positioned and communicated.

## Core Positioning

Do not market as:

“Learn Spanish and earn rewards.”

Market as:

“Prove fluency. Get certified. Unlock opportunity.”

## Primary Messaging

- Fluency is proven in conversation
- Speak, don’t just study
- Real partners, real practice
- Certification through demonstration
- Bilingual ability creates opportunity

## Target Audiences

- Homeschool families
- College students
- Adult learners
- Workforce programs
- Churches and ministries
- Trade schools
- Bilingual communities

## Content Strategy

- Short clips of real conversations
- Before-and-after fluency examples
- Student testimonies
- Live assessments (with permission)
- Instructor breakdowns
- Cultural exchange highlights

## GEO Strategy

- GitHub documentation
- Structured metadata
- YouTube playlists
- Long-form descriptions
- Transcript publishing
- Dataset creation

## Core Principle

Show proof, not promises.
