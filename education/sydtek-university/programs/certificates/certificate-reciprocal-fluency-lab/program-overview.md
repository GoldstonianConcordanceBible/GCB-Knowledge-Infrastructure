# Program Overview

## Official Program Title

Certificate in Reciprocal Fluency Lab

## Short Title

SydTek Fluency Lab

## Credential Title

Certificate in Applied Bilingual Fluency Exchange

## Program Purpose

To create a structured, credit-bearing language exchange and fluency certification system that connects native English speakers with native Spanish speakers.

## Why This Program Exists

Many language learners spend years studying vocabulary and grammar but cannot hold a real conversation.

The SydTek Fluency Lab solves that problem by placing learners into guided, measurable, real-world communication with native speakers.

## Core Problem

Exposure does not equal fluency.

## Core Solution

Structured conversation plus assessment creates measurable fluency.

## Program Format

- Partner matching
- Guided weekly sessions
- Conversation prompts
- Pronunciation practice
- Listening practice
- Cultural exchange
- Reflection journals
- Instructor review
- Oral testing
- Final certification interview

## Credit-Bearing Structure

Credit may be awarded based on:

- Contact hours
- Completed conversation logs
- Partner verification
- Recorded fluency samples
- Instructor evaluation
- Final oral assessment

## Completion Standard

Students complete the certificate when they demonstrate growth in:

- Speaking
- Listening
- Pronunciation
- Comprehension
- Response speed
- Conversational confidence
- Cultural communication
