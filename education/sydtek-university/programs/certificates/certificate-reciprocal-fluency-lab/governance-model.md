# Governance Model

## Purpose

This document defines governance for the SydTek Fluency Lab and its learned-and-earned model.

## Governance Goal

Protect the value of the certificate.

## Governing Bodies

### Program Director

Responsible for final academic and operational authority.

### Instructor Council

Reviews assessment standards, rubrics, and certification quality.

### Mentor Council

Represents verified peer mentors and advanced students.

### Governance Contributors

Verified participants who have earned reputation through service.

## Governance Responsibilities

Governance may oversee:

- Rubric updates
- Certification levels
- Mentor approval
- Reward policy
- Token guardrails
- Scholarship decisions
- Curriculum expansion
- Anti-farming enforcement

## Who Can Vote

Voting should be limited to verified contributors.

Suggested requirements:

- Completed certificate
- Reputation score of 4 or higher
- No active conduct violations
- Minimum service contribution

## What Cannot Be Voted Away

Governance cannot remove:

- Final oral assessment
- Instructor review
- Anti-farming controls
- Code of conduct
- Certificate-first principle
- Fluency, not exposure doctrine

## Emergency Authority

The Program Director may pause rewards, certification, or governance activity if abuse is detected.

## Core Principle

Governance exists to defend standards, not lower them.
