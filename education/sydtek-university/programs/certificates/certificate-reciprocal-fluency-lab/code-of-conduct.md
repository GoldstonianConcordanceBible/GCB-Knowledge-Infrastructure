# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

The Fluency Lab requires trust.

Students must feel safe practicing, making mistakes, and receiving correction.

## Required Conduct

Students must:

- Speak respectfully
- Avoid insults or mocking
- Honor cultural differences
- Protect privacy
- Arrive prepared
- Complete agreed session time
- Give useful correction
- Receive correction with maturity

## Prohibited Conduct

Students may not:

- Harass partners
- Record without permission
- Share private information
- Discriminate
- Use offensive language toward a partner
- Pressure a partner outside the program
- Submit false session logs

## Privacy Rule

No student may publish or share partner recordings without written permission.

## Removal Policy

A student may be removed for:

- Repeated missed sessions
- Disrespectful behavior
- Dishonest logs
- Unsafe communication
- Refusal to follow instructor guidance

## Core Principle

Fluency requires humility.
