# Reputation Score

## Purpose

The Reputation Score measures trust, consistency, and contribution inside the Proof-of-Fluency Network.

## Why Reputation Matters

Tokens can be farmed.

Certificates can be weakened if standards are low.

Reputation protects the system by showing who is reliable, helpful, and proven.

## Score Categories

### Reliability

Does the student attend sessions consistently?

### Fluency Growth

Is the student improving in speaking and listening?

### Correction Quality

Does the student give helpful correction?

### Correction Response

Does the student apply feedback?

### Partner Support

Does the student help their partner grow?

### Cultural Respect

Does the student communicate with humility and respect?

### Mentor Readiness

Is the student ready to help others?

## Suggested Scale

1 = Low trust  
2 = Developing trust  
3 = Reliable  
4 = Strong contributor  
5 = Mentor-level contributor  

## Reputation Uses

Reputation may determine:

- Mentor eligibility
- Advanced pod access
- Scholarship eligibility
- Instructor recommendation
- Reward eligibility
- Governance participation

## Non-Transferable

Reputation cannot be bought, sold, transferred, or delegated.

## Core Principle

Reputation is earned through repeated proof.
