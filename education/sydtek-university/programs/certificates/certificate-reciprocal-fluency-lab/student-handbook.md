# Student Handbook

## Welcome

Welcome to the SydTek Fluency Lab.

This program helps you build real language ability through live conversation with a native speaker.

## Core Rule

You must speak.

Watching videos is not enough.

Reading notes is not enough.

Fluency grows when you listen, respond, make mistakes, receive correction, and try again.

## Student Responsibilities

You are expected to:

- Attend scheduled sessions
- Speak in your target language
- Listen respectfully
- Correct kindly
- Receive correction humbly
- Complete session logs
- Submit reflections
- Participate in assessment

## Partner Respect

Your partner is not your servant.

Your partner is not your translator.

Your partner is your fluency teammate.

## Correction Rule

Correction must be:

- Respectful
- Specific
- Helpful
- Patient

## Required Student Materials

- Notebook or digital journal
- Session log template
- Vocabulary list
- Device for video or audio calls
- Access to assigned prompts

## Completion

You complete the certificate when your required sessions, logs, reflections, and final assessment are approved.
