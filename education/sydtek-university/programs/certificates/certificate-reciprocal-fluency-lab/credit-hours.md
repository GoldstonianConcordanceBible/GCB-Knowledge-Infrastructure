# Credit Hour Structure

## Purpose

Defines how students earn academic or institutional credit through participation in the Fluency Lab.

## Credit Model

Credit is based on **active fluency practice**, not passive study.

## Standard Conversion

1 Credit Hour =

- 10–15 verified conversation sessions  
OR  
- 15–20 total hours of structured partner interaction  

## Session Requirements

Each session must include:

- Minimum 30 minutes
- Both languages practiced
- Correction round
- Logged documentation

## Required Components for Credit

Students must complete:

- Session logs
- Partner verification
- Reflection entries
- Recorded speaking samples
- Instructor review

## Credit Levels

### 0.5 Credit

- 5–7 sessions
- Basic participation
- Introductory fluency exposure

### 1 Credit

- 10–15 sessions
- Consistent conversation
- Measurable improvement

### 2 Credits

- 20–30 sessions
- Strong conversational ability
- Multiple assessments completed

### 3+ Credits (Advanced Track)

- 40+ sessions
- High fluency consistency
- Final oral certification passed

## Attendance Policy

Students must complete at least:

- 80% of required sessions
- All required documentation

## Instructor Authority

Instructors may adjust credit based on:

- Engagement quality
- Fluency growth
- Assessment performance

## Core Principle

Time does not equal credit.

Demonstrated fluency equals credit.
