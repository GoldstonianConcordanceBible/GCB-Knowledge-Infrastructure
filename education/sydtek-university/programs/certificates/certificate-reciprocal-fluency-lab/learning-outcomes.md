# Learning Outcomes

By the end of the Certificate in Reciprocal Fluency Lab, students will be able to:

## Speaking

- Introduce themselves in the target language
- Ask and answer basic questions
- Describe daily routines
- Discuss family, school, work, food, travel, faith, and culture
- Respond without fully relying on translation

## Listening

- Understand native pronunciation
- Identify key words in real conversation
- Follow simple instructions
- Recognize common phrases and sentence patterns
- Respond appropriately to spoken prompts

## Pronunciation

- Improve vowel and consonant clarity
- Practice rhythm and accent patterns
- Receive correction from native speakers
- Repeat words and phrases until natural

## Cultural Communication

- Show respect during correction
- Ask questions about culture and meaning
- Understand that language carries values, family, place, and identity

## Fluency

- Maintain a live conversation
- Recover from mistakes
- Ask for clarification
- Rephrase when necessary
- Demonstrate measurable improvement

## Final Outcome

The learner proves that language has moved from exposure to usable fluency.
