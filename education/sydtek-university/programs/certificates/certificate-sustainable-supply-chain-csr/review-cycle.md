# Review Cycle
## Certificate in Sustainable Supply Chain Management & Corporate Social Responsibility

This document provides a recommended review schedule for program maintenance, quality assurance, and improvement.

---

## Purpose

This certificate should be reviewed regularly to ensure that it remains:

- academically coherent
- instructionally usable
- technologically current
- professionally relevant
- aligned with SydTek University and SydTek Scholars goals

---

## Recommended Review Schedule

### Every Term or Delivery Cycle
Program leadership or instructors should review:

- assignment clarity
- rubric usefulness
- student workload balance
- portfolio quality
- common student failure points

### Every 6 Months
Program leadership should review:

- course content drift
- outdated terminology
- case-study relevance
- technology references
- faculty feedback
- student performance patterns

### Every 12 Months
A fuller review should examine:

- program learning outcomes
- assessment quality
- capstone strength
- market relevance
- follow-on pathway fit
- possible revisions to sequencing or scope

---

## What Should Be Reviewed

### Curriculum
- course descriptions
- weekly topics
- learning outcomes
- assignment design

### Assessment
- rubric clarity
- alignment to outcomes
- portfolio evidence quality
- consistency across instructors

### Relevance
- supply chain trends
- sustainability expectations
- governance language
- technology use cases
- reporting and disclosure concepts

### Delivery
- GitHub usability
- student orientation needs
- faculty support needs
- scholars adaptation effectiveness

---

## Review Questions

Program reviewers should ask:

1. Are students producing the kind of work the certificate promises?
2. Are assessments actually measuring judgment and application?
3. Are students overusing jargon without operational understanding?
4. Are technology discussions still current and grounded?
5. Does the capstone still function as a true synthesis point?
6. Are portfolio artifacts strong enough for outside review?

---

## Recommended Review Outputs

After each formal review cycle, leadership should produce a short internal note covering:

- what is working
- what needs revision
- what should be added
- what should be retired
- what should be reviewed again next cycle

---

## Versioning Practice

If the certificate changes meaningfully, leadership should update:

- README
- PROGRAM_GUIDE
- curriculum.yaml
- relevant course files
- alignment documents

A dated revision note is recommended for major updates.

---

## Final Principle

A strong certificate is not static.

It remains stable in mission while being willing to refine language, cases, tools, and evidence practices as the field evolves.
