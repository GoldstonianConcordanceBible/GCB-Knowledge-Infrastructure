# Portfolio Submission Guide
## Certificate in Sustainable Supply Chain Management & Corporate Social Responsibility

This guide explains how students should prepare and submit their final portfolio artifacts.

---

## Purpose of the Portfolio

The portfolio is the visible evidence layer of the certificate.

Its purpose is to show that you can:

- understand systems
- identify risk
- reason ethically
- evaluate technology
- communicate strategically
- recommend practical improvements

This portfolio should be understandable to faculty and useful for outside review.

---

## Required Artifacts

Students should submit at least the following:

1. **SSCM 101 Artifact**  
   A revised supply chain map and explanation

2. **SSCM 201 Artifact**  
   A revised CSR or stakeholder governance memo

3. **SSCM 301 Artifact**  
   A revised technology readiness or recommendation brief

4. **SSCM 401 Artifact**  
   The final sustainable supply chain strategy

---

## Revision Expectation

Do not simply upload first-draft work.

Before adding an artifact to the portfolio, revise it for:

- clarity
- structure
- reasoning
- professionalism
- grammar and formatting
- stronger recommendations where needed
