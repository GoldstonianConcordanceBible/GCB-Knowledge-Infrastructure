# Program Learning Outcomes Matrix
## Certificate in Sustainable Supply Chain Management & Corporate Social Responsibility

This matrix shows where each program learning outcome is introduced, reinforced, and mastered across the certificate.

---

## Key

- **I** = Introduced
- **R** = Reinforced
- **M** = Mastered

---

---

## Interpretation Notes

### SSCM 101
Focuses on introduction and baseline competence.
Students begin the certificate by learning the language, structure, and basic logic of sustainable supply chain systems.

### SSCM 201
Reinforces the program by deepening ethical, labor, governance, and stakeholder reasoning.

### SSCM 301
Builds stronger judgment in the area of technological fit, transparency systems, and digital decision support.

### SSCM 401
Functions as the mastery course in which all major outcomes should be demonstrated together in integrated form.

---

## Program Design Note

This matrix reflects intentional scaffolding.
No single course is expected to carry the entire certificate.
Mastery is expected at the capstone level, not at the point of first exposure.
