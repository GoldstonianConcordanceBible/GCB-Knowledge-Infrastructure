# Model SSCM 401 Artifact
## Executive Summary Example

**Project:** Sustainable Supply Chain Strategy for a Regional Hospital Network  
**Course:** SSCM 401 — Capstone: Sustainable Supply Chain Strategy Lab

---

The hospital network faces a high-risk combination of inventory visibility gaps, inconsistent supplier performance, and limited system-wide coordination across sites. These issues create operational fragility and weaken resilience during periods of demand volatility.

The recommended strategy is phased rather than totalizing.

Phase 1 should focus on critical inventory visibility, vendor-risk ranking, and emergency supply continuity procedures.
Phase 2 should strengthen supplier accountability, reporting standards, and cross-site coordination.
Phase 3 should introduce targeted technology support where it improves forecasting, traceability, and decision-making without creating unnecessary complexity.

This strategy is preferable to a full-system overhaul because it addresses the most urgent failures first while preserving organizational capacity for implementation.

The success of the redesign should be measured through a small set of KPIs tied to stockout reduction, supplier performance stability, response speed, and visibility coverage.

The central principle is simple:
resilience improves when visibility, governance, and prioritization improve together.
