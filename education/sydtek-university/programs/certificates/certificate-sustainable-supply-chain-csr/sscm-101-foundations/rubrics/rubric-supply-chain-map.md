# Rubric — End-to-End Supply Chain Map

## Criteria

### 1. Completeness of Map
- Excellent: full end-to-end structure with strong detail
- Proficient: mostly complete with minor gaps
- Developing: partial structure with important omissions
- Beginning: major gaps or confusion

### 2. Risk Identification
- Excellent: accurately identifies environmental, ethical, and resilience risks
- Proficient: identifies most major risks
- Developing: identifies some risks but misses major concerns
- Beginning: weak or inaccurate risk identification

### 3. Analysis Quality
- Excellent: sharp, practical, systems-based reasoning
- Proficient: clear and logical analysis
- Developing: general observations with limited depth
- Beginning: unsupported or unclear claims

### 4. Redesign Recommendation
- Excellent: realistic and strategic
- Proficient: useful and relevant
- Developing: somewhat relevant but underdeveloped
- Beginning: vague or impractical
