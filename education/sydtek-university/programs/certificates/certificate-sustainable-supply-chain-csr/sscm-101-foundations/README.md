# SSCM 101 — Foundations of Sustainable Supply Chains

## Course Overview

SSCM 101 introduces learners to the structure, purpose, and vulnerabilities of modern supply chains through the lens of sustainability and resilience.

Students learn that supply chains are not only about moving goods efficiently. They are also about people, ethics, environmental impact, continuity, stakeholder trust, and long-term organizational survival.

This course serves as the foundation for the entire certificate.

---

## Course Description

This course examines the basic components of supply chain management, including sourcing, procurement, logistics, warehousing, fulfillment, and coordination across partners. It introduces sustainability as a practical operating principle rather than a public relations slogan.

Students will explore how organizations manage tradeoffs between cost, speed, resilience, transparency, and responsibility.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, learners will be able to:

1. Define sustainable supply chain management.
2. Explain the major components of an end-to-end supply chain.
3. Identify environmental, social, and operational risks.
4. Map supplier, logistics, and customer relationships.
5. Distinguish short-term optimization from long-term resilience.
6. Evaluate how disruption affects supply chain performance.

## Signature Assignments

### 1. End-to-End Supply Chain Map
Students map a product or service from origin to customer delivery and identify:
- suppliers
- logistics stages
- environmental hotspots
- labor and ethical risk points
- resilience concerns

### 2. Disruption Analysis Memo
Students analyze a disruption scenario and explain:
- what failed
- who was affected
- what could have reduced the damage
- how sustainability and resilience overlap

### 3. Sustainability Maturity Reflection
Students evaluate whether an organization is:
- reactive
- compliant
- strategic
- transformational

## Discussion Prompts
