# Assignment 01 — End-to-End Supply Chain Map

## Objective

Map a product or service from origin to customer delivery and identify the major sustainability, ethical, and resilience risks across the chain.

## Instructions

Choose one of the following:

- a physical consumer product
- a healthcare product
- a food product
- a digital service with infrastructure dependencies
- an approved alternative

Create a map showing:

- raw material or source origin
- supplier tiers if known
- manufacturing or service delivery stages
- transportation and warehousing points
- customer delivery stage
- reverse logistics or waste stage if relevant

Then add a written explanation covering:

- the most fragile point in the chain
- the highest ethical or labor-risk point
- the largest environmental concern
- the point where visibility is weakest
- one redesign recommendation

## Deliverable

- one visual map
- one 750 to 1000 word explanation

## Evaluation Focus

- completeness
- systems thinking
- risk identification
- practical reasoning
- clarity
