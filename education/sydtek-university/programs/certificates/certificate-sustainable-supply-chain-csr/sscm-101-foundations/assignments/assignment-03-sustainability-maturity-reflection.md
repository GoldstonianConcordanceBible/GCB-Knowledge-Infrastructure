# Assignment 03 — Sustainability Maturity Reflection

## Objective

Evaluate an organization’s current sustainability posture and determine whether it is reactive, compliant, strategic, or transformational.

## Instructions

Select an organization and assess:

- whether sustainability is visible in operations
- whether responsibility appears embedded or symbolic
- whether suppliers and stakeholders are meaningfully considered
- whether resilience is built into the system
- whether public messaging matches operational reality

## Deliverable

- 800 to 1000 word reflection

## Required Conclusion

End by assigning the organization one maturity category:

- reactive
- compliant
- strategic
- transformational

Defend your classification with evidence and reasoning.
