# Assignment 02 — Disruption Analysis Memo

## Objective

Analyze a real or hypothetical supply chain disruption and explain what failed, why it mattered, and what changes could reduce future damage.

## Prompt

Write a memo that addresses:

- what disruption occurred
- which actors were affected
- where the bottleneck or failure emerged
- how the disruption spread through the system
- what resilience measures could have reduced impact
- how sustainability and resilience connect in this case

## Deliverable

- 1000 to 1200 word memo

## Recommended Structure

1. disruption summary
2. impact analysis
3. root cause discussion
4. resilience lessons
5. sustainability implications
6. final recommendations
