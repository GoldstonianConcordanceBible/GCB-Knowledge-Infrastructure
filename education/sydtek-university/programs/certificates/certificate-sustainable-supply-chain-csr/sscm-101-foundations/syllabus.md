# SSCM 101 Syllabus

## Course Title
SSCM 101 — Foundations of Sustainable Supply Chains

## Course Length
8 weeks

## Credit Value
3 credit hours university track  
Scholars equivalent: Module 1

## Prerequisites
None

## Required Mindset
Students should be prepared to think systemically, ethically, and strategically.

## Assessment Breakdown
- discussions — 15%
- supply chain map — 25%
- disruption memo — 20%
- sustainability reflection — 15%
- final synthesis assignment — 25%

## Course Materials
Instructor-provided case studies, articles, videos, and organizational examples.

## Completion Standard
Students must demonstrate the ability to map and evaluate a supply chain through operational and sustainability lenses.
