# SSCM 401 — Capstone: Sustainable Supply Chain Strategy Lab

## Course Overview

SSCM 401 is the culminating course of the certificate. Students integrate sustainability, CSR, governance, resilience, and technology into a practical transformation strategy for a real or hypothetical organization.

This course is where theory becomes institutional design.

---

## Course Description

In this capstone, students diagnose an organization’s supply chain and develop a sustainable improvement strategy grounded in operational reality. They produce a transformation roadmap that addresses risk, stakeholder expectations, traceability, reporting readiness, and long-term resilience.

The final deliverable is designed to function as a portfolio artifact for academic, consulting, or employer review.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, learners will be able to:

1. Diagnose a supply chain from multiple risk perspectives.
2. Prioritize sustainability and responsibility interventions.
3. Build an implementation roadmap.
4. Recommend appropriate technology support.
5. Design practical KPIs and reporting structures.
6. Communicate recommendations to executive stakeholders.

## Weekly Structure

### Week 1 — Selecting the Organization
- sector choice
- organizational profile
- scope of analysis
- available data

### Week 2 — Current-State Diagnosis
- mapping the chain
- identifying weak points
- supplier and logistics review
- visibility assessment

### Week 3 — Risk Matrix Development
- operational risk
- ethical risk
- environmental risk
- regulatory risk
- reputational risk

### Week 4 — Sustainability and CSR Intervention Planning
- sourcing improvements
- labor and governance interventions
- reporting priorities
- accountability structures

### Week 5 — Technology and Data Layer
- choosing the right systems
- implementation sequence
- information requirements
- transparency design

### Week 6 — KPI and Reporting Design
- what to measure
- how often to measure
- who owns the data
- what stakeholders need to see

### Week 7 — Executive Communication
- writing for decision-makers
- presenting tradeoffs
- implementation realism
- change management

### Week 8 — Final Presentation and Portfolio Submission
- transformation roadmap
- final strategy presentation
- GitHub portfolio packaging

---

## Signature Assignments

### 1. Capstone Proposal
Students choose an organization and define:
- scope
- problem statement
- data assumptions
- intended outcome

### 2. Current-State Risk Map
Students create a risk map covering:
- operational fragility
- labor and sourcing concerns
- environmental impact
- governance gaps
- compliance pressure

### 3. Final Sustainable Supply Chain Strategy
Students produce a complete plan including:
- diagnosis
- prioritized interventions
- implementation phases
- technology recommendations
- KPIs
- governance notes
- executive summary
