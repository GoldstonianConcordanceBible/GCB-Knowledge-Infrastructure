# SSCM 401 Syllabus

## Course Title
SSCM 401 — Capstone: Sustainable Supply Chain Strategy Lab

## Course Length
8 weeks

## Credit Value
3 credit hours university track  
Scholars equivalent: Module 4

## Prerequisites
SSCM 101  
SSCM 201  
SSCM 301

## Assessment Breakdown
- capstone proposal — 15%
- current-state risk map — 20%
- discussion and workshop participation — 15%
- interim strategy draft — 20%
- final capstone strategy — 30%

## Course Focus
This course emphasizes integration, execution, and executive communication.

## Completion Standard
Students must submit a defensible supply chain transformation strategy that demonstrates synthesis of the entire certificate.
