# Case 02 — Hospital Supply Chain Redesign

## Summary

A regional hospital network faces recurring delays in critical supplies, poor visibility into inventory levels across sites, inconsistent vendor performance, and growing pressure to improve sustainability and reporting practices. Leadership wants a strategy that improves resilience without creating unmanageable complexity.

## Learning Purpose

This case helps students integrate:

- operational diagnosis
- risk prioritization
- ethics and stewardship
- technology evaluation
- phased implementation
- executive communication

## Key Questions

1. What is the most urgent risk in this system?
2. Which changes should happen immediately, and which should wait?
3. What technology would genuinely help here?
4. What KPIs should leadership track first?
5. How should the transformation be phased?
