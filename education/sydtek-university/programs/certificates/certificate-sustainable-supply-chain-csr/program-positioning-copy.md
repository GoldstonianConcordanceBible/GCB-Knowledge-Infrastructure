# Program Positioning Copy

## Short Description
The Certificate in Sustainable Supply Chain Management & Corporate Social Responsibility prepares learners to build transparent, resilient, and ethically grounded supply chains. Students study sustainability, CSR, stakeholder governance, AI, blockchain, digital twins, and reporting readiness in a practical online format designed for the AI age.

## Catalog Description
This certificate equips students with applied knowledge in sustainable operations, corporate social responsibility, supply chain resilience, and digital transformation. Learners examine how organizations balance cost, transparency, ethics, environmental accountability, and long-term strategic value. The curriculum integrates case-based learning with practical tools for stakeholder mapping, supplier evaluation, emissions awareness, compliance readiness, and technology-enabled visibility.

## SydTek Scholars Description
SydTek Scholars students in this pathway learn how products move through the world, why ethics and sustainability matter in business, and how modern technologies can improve transparency and resilience. The program is designed for advanced students who want college-level exposure to business, logistics, ESG thinking, and leadership.
