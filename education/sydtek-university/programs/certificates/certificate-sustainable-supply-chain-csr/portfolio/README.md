# Rubric — Final Sustainable Supply Chain Strategy

## Criteria

### 1. Diagnostic Quality
The student accurately understands the organization and its supply chain realities.

### 2. Strategic Integration
The plan integrates sustainability, CSR, resilience, technology, and governance.

### 3. Prioritization
The proposed interventions are sequenced intelligently and realistically.

### 4. Executive Readiness
The final work is structured in a way leaders could actually use.

### 5. Depth and Practicality
The submission moves beyond ideals and demonstrates implementation thinking.
