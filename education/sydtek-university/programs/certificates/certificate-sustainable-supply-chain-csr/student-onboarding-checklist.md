# Student Onboarding Checklist
## Certificate in Sustainable Supply Chain Management & Corporate Social Responsibility

Use this checklist at the beginning of the certificate.

---

## Getting Started

- Read the main program README
- Read the student orientation file
- Review the course syllabus
- Review the major assignments
- Review the grading rubrics
- Understand which artifacts belong in the final portfolio

---

## Mindset Checklist

- I understand this program is about systems, not just shipping
- I understand sustainability must be operational, not symbolic
- I understand technology should be evaluated critically
- I understand realism matters more than buzzwords
- I understand writing quality is part of professional competence

---

## Workflow Checklist

- I know where course files are stored
- I know where to submit or save my artifacts
- I know how my portfolio should be organized
- I know which assignment in each course is most likely to become a portfolio artifact
- I know where to find the capstone template if applicable

---

## Success Checklist

- I will revise before adding work to my portfolio
- I will explain my reasoning clearly
- I will connect ethics to operations
- I will avoid vague recommendations
- I will treat my best work as professional evidence, not just classwork
