# Admissions
## Certificate in Sustainable Supply Chain Management & Corporate Social Responsibility

This document provides recommended admissions guidance for the certificate.

---

## General Eligibility

The certificate is designed to be accessible to a wide range of learners.

Recommended applicants include:

- current university students
- adult learners
- workforce transition learners
- early-career professionals
- advanced homeschool students
- independent scholars with strong writing ability

---

## University Track Admission

Recommended entry profile:

- high school completion or equivalent
- interest in business, operations, sustainability, ethics, or technology
- ability to read analytical material
- ability to complete written assignments independently

No prior supply chain experience is required.

---

## SydTek Scholars Admission

Recommended entry profile:

- strong reading and writing ability
- maturity for analytical and ethical discussion
- interest in business, technology, stewardship, or systems thinking
- readiness for honors-level independent work

Recommended grade range:
- advanced secondary learners

---

## Ideal Student Traits

Students are likely to succeed if they are:

- curious about how systems work
- willing to think in cause-and-effect terms
- open to ethical and practical analysis
- able to complete structured writing assignments
- interested in real-world problem solving

---

## Suggested Placement Guidance

### Best Fit
- learners interested in business systems
- learners interested in supply chain, operations, or sustainability
- learners exploring ESG, governance, or responsible leadership
- learners interested in AI and digital transformation in real organizations

### Not Ideal Without Support
- learners who strongly resist writing
- learners who need highly hands-on technical training only
- learners seeking a purely mathematical operations curriculum

---

## Prerequisites

Formal prerequisites are minimal.

Recommended readiness includes:

- basic writing ability
- basic digital literacy
- willingness to use GitHub or a similar repository-based workflow
- ability to work through case-based learning

---

## Transfer and Prior Learning Notes

Program leadership may choose to recognize prior learning where a student can demonstrate:

- relevant work experience
- completed supply chain or business coursework
- portfolio evidence of comparable analysis
- equivalent project-based experience

---

## Admissions Positioning Statement

This certificate is intentionally broad enough to serve multiple learner populations while remaining rigorous in analysis, writing, and portfolio production.

Its strength is not narrow technical specialization.
Its strength is integrated judgment.
