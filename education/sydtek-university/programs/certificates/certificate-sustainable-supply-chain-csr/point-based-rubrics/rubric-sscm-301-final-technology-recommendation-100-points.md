# Point-Based Rubric
## SSCM 301 — Final Technology Recommendation Brief
## Total: 100 Points

### 1. Problem Definition — 20 points
Evaluates whether the operational problem is clearly identified.

- 18–20: precise, realistic, well-scoped problem definition
- 15–17: clear overall with minor gaps
- 11–14: somewhat clear but underdeveloped
- 0–10: vague or poorly framed

### 2. Technology Fit — 25 points
Evaluates how well the recommendation matches the real need.

- 22–25: excellent fit, strong judgment, problem-first reasoning
- 18–21: solid fit with reasonable logic
- 14–17: partially relevant but somewhat tool-first
- 0–13: weak fit or hype-driven reasoning

### 3. Governance and Risk Awareness — 20 points
Evaluates treatment of data quality, oversight, implementation risk, and constraints.

- 18–20: strong governance awareness and realistic risk framing
- 15–17: good recognition of major governance needs
- 11–14: some risk awareness but incomplete
- 0–10: weak treatment of governance or risk

### 4. Practicality and Sequencing — 20 points
Evaluates realism of implementation logic.

- 18–20: realistic, phased, and organization-aware
- 15–17: mostly practical with minor gaps
- 11–14: somewhat practical but underdeveloped
- 0–10: unrealistic or poorly sequenced

### 5. Communication Quality — 15 points
Evaluates organization, clarity, and persuasiveness.

- 13–15: clear, concise, persuasive
- 10–12: understandable and organized
- 7–9: uneven clarity or structure
- 0–6: unclear or poorly communicated
