# Point-Based Rubric
## SSCM 101 — End-to-End Supply Chain Map
## Total: 100 Points

### 1. Completeness of Map — 25 points
Evaluates whether the student mapped the chain in a full and coherent way.

- 22–25: comprehensive, logically organized, strong end-to-end visibility
- 18–21: mostly complete, minor gaps
- 14–17: partially complete, several missing links
- 0–13: weak, fragmented, or unclear

### 2. Risk Identification — 25 points
Evaluates recognition of environmental, ethical, operational, and visibility risks.

- 22–25: sharp and well-balanced risk analysis
- 18–21: solid identification of most major risks
- 14–17: some valid points but notable gaps
- 0–13: weak, vague, or inaccurate risk analysis

### 3. Explanation Quality — 25 points
Evaluates clarity, systems thinking, and practical reasoning in the written explanation.

- 22–25: clear, insightful, realistic, well-supported
- 18–21: organized and logical with moderate depth
- 14–17: general explanation with limited precision
- 0–13: unclear or underdeveloped explanation

### 4. Redesign Recommendation — 15 points
Evaluates whether the proposed improvement is realistic and useful.

- 13–15: practical, strategic, clearly connected to the analysis
- 10–12: relevant and mostly realistic
- 7–9: somewhat relevant but underdeveloped
- 0–6: vague, weak, or disconnected

### 5. Professional Presentation — 10 points
Evaluates organization, readability, formatting, and professionalism.

- 9–10: polished, well-structured, easy to follow
- 7–8: clear overall with minor presentation issues
- 5–6: uneven structure or readability
- 0–4: difficult to follow or poorly presented
