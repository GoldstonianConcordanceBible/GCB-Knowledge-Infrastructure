# Point-Based Rubric
## SSCM 201 — Board Memo on Stakeholder Risk
## Total: 100 Points

### 1. Risk Framing — 20 points
Evaluates whether the memo clearly explains the nature of the responsibility-related risk.

- 18–20: clear, urgent, well-framed risk definition
- 15–17: solid framing with minor gaps
- 11–14: somewhat clear but incomplete
- 0–10: weak or confusing framing

### 2. Stakeholder and Governance Analysis — 25 points
Evaluates identification of affected stakeholders and underlying governance weaknesses.

- 22–25: precise stakeholder analysis and strong governance diagnosis
- 18–21: good analysis with moderate depth
- 14–17: partial but uneven analysis
- 0–13: shallow or unclear analysis

### 3. Actionability of Recommendations — 25 points
Evaluates whether the memo gives realistic and useful next steps.

- 22–25: strong, prioritized, executive-usable actions
- 18–21: useful recommendations with reasonable logic
- 14–17: some relevant actions but limited prioritization
- 0–13: vague or impractical actions

### 4. Executive Tone and Structure — 20 points
Evaluates whether the memo is concise, decision-maker oriented, and well organized.

- 18–20: highly professional, concise, executive-ready
- 15–17: mostly effective with minor issues
- 11–14: some memo structure but inconsistent tone
- 0–10: not written for an executive audience

### 5. Writing Quality — 10 points
Evaluates readability, grammar, and clarity.

- 9–10: polished and clear
- 7–8: minor issues only
- 5–6: noticeable writing problems
- 0–4: significant readability problems
