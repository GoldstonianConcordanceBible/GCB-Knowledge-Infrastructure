# Case Study Library
## Certificate in Sustainable Supply Chain Management & Corporate Social Responsibility

This folder is the case-study bank for the certificate.

It should grow over time.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of this library is to support applied teaching.

Students learn this material best when they work through concrete examples rather than only abstract definitions.

Cases should help students practice:

- supply chain mapping
- disruption analysis
- labor and ethics reasoning
- governance diagnosis
- technology evaluation
- transformation planning

---

## Recommended Case Categories

### 1. Disruption Cases
Examples involving:

- transportation bottlenecks
- supplier shutdowns
- shortages
- delays
- geopolitical shocks
- disaster-related disruption

### 2. Ethics and Labor Cases
Examples involving:

- unsafe working conditions
- forced labor risk
- wage issues
- supplier abuse
- weak oversight
- failure of accountability systems

### 3. Sustainability Cases
Examples involving:

- emissions-heavy operations
- waste reduction failure
- unsustainable sourcing
- packaging redesign
- circularity efforts
- environmental controversy

### 4. Governance Cases
Examples involving:

- corruption
- procurement misconduct
- weak controls
- misleading public claims
- disclosure failure
- reputation collapse

### 5. Technology Cases
Examples involving:

- AI forecasting systems
- blockchain traceability pilots
- IoT monitoring
- digital twins
- dashboard misuse
- poor data quality

### 6. Transformation Cases
Examples involving:

- successful redesign
- phased implementation
- resilience investment
- supplier engagement strategy
- technology integration
- KPI and reporting improvement
