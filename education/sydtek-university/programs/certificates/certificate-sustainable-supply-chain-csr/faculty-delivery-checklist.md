# Faculty Delivery Checklist
## Certificate in Sustainable Supply Chain Management & Corporate Social Responsibility

Use this checklist before and during delivery of the certificate.

---

## Before the Course Starts

- Confirm course README and syllabus are present and current
- Confirm all major assignment files are present
- Confirm rubrics are present and match the assignments
- Confirm discussion prompts are ready
- Confirm at least one case study is selected for the term
- Confirm portfolio expectations are explained
- Confirm capstone expectations are explained if teaching SSCM 401
- Confirm GitHub folder structure is clean and usable
- Confirm any local policy adaptations are documented

---

## During the Course

- Introduce the course purpose clearly in week 1
- Reinforce systems thinking, realism, and ethical seriousness
- Redirect students away from buzzwords toward specifics
- Provide feedback that is clear and actionable
- Watch for unrealistic recommendations
- Watch for tool-first reasoning in SSCM 301 and SSCM 401
- Save examples of common strengths and weaknesses for moderation
- Remind students to revise portfolio artifacts, not just submit drafts

---

## Mid-Course Review

- Check whether students understand assignment expectations
- Check whether rubric language is being applied consistently
- Identify recurring areas of confusion
- Adjust scaffolding where needed
- Confirm pacing is manageable

---

## End-of-Course Review

- Confirm major assessments are complete
- Confirm portfolio artifacts are identified
- Confirm grading comments are saved where helpful for moderation
- Note which cases and prompts worked best
- Record recommended revisions for next delivery cycle

---

## Program-Level Good Practice

- Treat the capstone as a synthesis project, not a loose essay
- Reward realism more than inflated ambition
- Reward clarity more than jargon
- Preserve consistent terminology across sections
- Document meaningful updates in RELEASE_NOTES.md
