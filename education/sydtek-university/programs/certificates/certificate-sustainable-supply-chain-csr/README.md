## SydTek University × SydTek Scholars

This certificate prepares learners to design, evaluate, and improve supply chains through the lenses of sustainability, corporate social responsibility (CSR), resilience, transparency, and emerging technology.

The program is grounded in the public scholarship and teaching themes associated with Dr. Justin Goldston’s work in supply chain management, sustainability, CSR, AI, blockchain, and digital transformation.

## Program Purpose

Modern supply chains are no longer judged only by cost and speed.
They are judged by:

- resilience
- transparency
- environmental impact
- ethical sourcing
- labor and human-rights practices
- stakeholder trust
- digital traceability
- regulatory readiness

This certificate equips students to lead in that environment.

## Core Learning Outcomes

By the end of the program, learners will be able to:

1. Explain the relationship between sustainability, CSR, and supply chain strategy.
2. Evaluate supply chain risk through environmental, social, operational, and regulatory lenses.
3. Map supply chain stakeholders, flows, and weak points.
4. Assess transparency and traceability practices across suppliers and logistics partners.
5. Interpret the role of AI, blockchain, analytics, and digital twins in resilient supply chains.
6. Analyze emissions, ethics, labor, and governance issues at a practical level.
7. Build a basic sustainable supply chain improvement plan.
8. Communicate findings in business, academic, and public-facing formats.
