# Assignment 02 — Supplier Ethics Case Brief

## Objective

Analyze a supplier ethics failure and recommend a governance response.

## Prompt

Choose a real or realistic supplier ethics issue involving one or more of the following:

- labor abuse
- unsafe conditions
- sourcing opacity
- environmental negligence
- bribery or procurement misconduct

Explain:

- what happened
- who was responsible
- which controls failed
- how trust was damaged
- what corrective action should occur
- how recurrence can be reduced

## Deliverable

- 1000 to 1300 word case brief
