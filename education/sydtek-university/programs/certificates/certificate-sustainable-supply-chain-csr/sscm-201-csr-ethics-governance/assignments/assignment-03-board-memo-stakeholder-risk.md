# Assignment 03 — Board Memo on Stakeholder Risk

## Objective

Write a board-facing memo explaining a responsibility-related risk that could damage the organization.

## Memo Must Address

- the nature of the risk
- affected stakeholders
- likely reputational and operational consequences
- governance weakness
- priority action steps
- communication considerations

## Deliverable

- 800 to 1000 word executive memo

## Tone Requirement

Write as if senior leaders must act on your memo this week.
