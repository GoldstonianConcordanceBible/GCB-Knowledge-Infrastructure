# Assignment 01 — CSR Scorecard

## Objective

Evaluate an organization’s CSR posture across core dimensions of responsibility.

## Scorecard Categories

Students must assess:

- labor and workforce treatment
- supplier responsibility
- environmental accountability
- governance and anti-corruption
- public transparency
- stakeholder communication
- crisis response credibility

## Deliverable

- one scorecard
- one 900 to 1200 word explanation

## Required Conclusion

State whether the organization’s CSR appears:

- symbolic
- developing
- operationalized
- strategically embedded
