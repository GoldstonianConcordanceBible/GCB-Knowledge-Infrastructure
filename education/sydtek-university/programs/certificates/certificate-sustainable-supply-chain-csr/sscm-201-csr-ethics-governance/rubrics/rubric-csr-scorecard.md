# Rubric — CSR Scorecard

## Criteria

### 1. Breadth of Evaluation
Assesses whether the student meaningfully covers the required CSR dimensions.

### 2. Ethical Judgment
Assesses whether the student can distinguish symbolic responsibility from embedded responsibility.

### 3. Evidence and Reasoning
Assesses whether claims are supported and logically connected.

### 4. Practical Conclusion
Assesses whether the final judgment is defensible and useful.
