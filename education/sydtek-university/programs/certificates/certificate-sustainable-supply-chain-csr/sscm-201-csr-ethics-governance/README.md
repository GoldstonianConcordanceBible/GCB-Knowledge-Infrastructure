# SSCM 201 Syllabus

## Course Title
SSCM 201 — Corporate Social Responsibility, Ethics, and Stakeholder Governance

## Course Length
8 weeks

## Credit Value
3 credit hours university track  
Scholars equivalent: Module 2

## Prerequisites
SSCM 101 recommended

## Assessment Breakdown
- discussions — 15%
- CSR scorecard — 20%
- supplier ethics case brief — 20%
- board memo — 20%
- final analysis assignment — 25%

## Course Focus
This course emphasizes ethical reasoning, governance awareness, and stakeholder-centered operations.

## Completion Standard
Students must demonstrate the ability to assess CSR and governance issues in an applied supply chain context.
