# Case 01 — Apparel Supplier Labor Abuse Allegation

## Summary

A clothing brand promotes ethical sourcing in its marketing, but an investigation alleges that one overseas supplier relies on excessive overtime, wage withholding, and unsafe working conditions. The company claims the facility was operated by a subcontractor outside its approved vendor list.

## Learning Purpose

This case helps students analyze:

- supplier accountability
- indirect responsibility
- monitoring failure
- public trust
- symbolic versus embedded CSR

## Key Questions

1. Does the subcontractor excuse the brand from responsibility?
2. What control systems failed?
3. What should leadership do in the next seven days?
4. What long-term governance reforms are needed?
