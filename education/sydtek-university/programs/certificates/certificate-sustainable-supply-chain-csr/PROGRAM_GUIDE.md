# Program Guide
## Certificate in Sustainable Supply Chain Management & Corporate Social Responsibility

This guide explains how the certificate works, who it is for, what students will produce, and how the program should be used within SydTek University and SydTek Scholars.

---

## 1. Program Purpose

The purpose of this certificate is to help learners understand how supply chains operate in the real world and how they can be improved through sustainability, corporate responsibility, resilience planning, and emerging technology.

This is not a narrow logistics certificate.
It is a systems-thinking certificate.

Students will study:

- operations
- sourcing
- stakeholder governance
- labor and ethics
- transparency
- resilience
- environmental impact
- AI and digital infrastructure
- executive communication

---

## 2. Intended Learners

This certificate is designed for:

- undergraduate students
- adult learners
- independent professionals
- advanced high school scholars
- future consultants
- future ESG or sustainability analysts
- future operations leaders

---

## 3. Program Philosophy

The program is built on five assumptions:

### A. Supply chains are moral systems as well as operational systems.
They affect workers, communities, environments, and public trust.

### B. Sustainability must be operationalized.
It cannot remain a slogan or branding exercise.

### C. Transparency and resilience are strategic assets.
Organizations that cannot see or explain their own systems are fragile.

### D. Technology is only useful when matched to real needs.
AI, blockchain, IoT, and digital twins are tools, not magic.

### E. Evidence matters.
Students should graduate from the certificate with visible, reviewable artifacts.

---

## 4. Program Completion Standards

To complete the certificate, students must:

- complete all four courses or aligned scholars modules
- pass major assignments
- submit portfolio artifacts
- complete the capstone strategy project
- demonstrate clear written reasoning and applied judgment

---

## 5. Signature Student Outputs

Students should finish the program with work that shows they can:

- map systems
- identify risk
- reason ethically
- assess governance
- evaluate technology
- propose realistic interventions
- communicate strategically

---

## 6. Recommended Pace

### Standard Pace
- 1 course every 8 weeks
- 32 total weeks for a full sequential experience

### Accelerated Pace
- 2 courses at a time where appropriate
- 16 to 20 weeks total depending on overlap and readiness

### Scholars Pace
- 1 module every 3 to 4 weeks
- 12 to 16 weeks total

---

## 7. Course Sequence

1. SSCM 101 — Foundations of Sustainable Supply Chains
2. SSCM 201 — CSR, Ethics, and Stakeholder Governance
3. SSCM 301 — Technology for Transparency and Resilience
4. SSCM 401 — Capstone Strategy Lab

This sequence should normally be preserved.

---

## 8. Assessment Philosophy

Assessment in this program should prioritize:

- clarity
- practical reasoning
- systems thinking
- ethical seriousness
- portfolio quality
- strategic realism

The program should not reward jargon without understanding.

---

## 9. Scholars Adaptation Notes

For SydTek Scholars, instructors may adapt:

- assignment length
- reading complexity
- case-study difficulty
- presentation format

However, the core ideas should remain intact:

- responsibility
- resilience
- transparency
- technology judgment
- practical leadership

---

## 10. Recommended Delivery Assets

Each course should eventually include:

- lectures or playlist links
- slides
- discussion prompts
- case studies
- rubrics
- faculty notes
- portfolio examples

---

## 11. Suggested Review Cycle

Program leadership should review the certificate every 6 to 12 months for:

- curriculum drift
- outdated terminology
- technology relevance
- regulatory relevance
- employer relevance
- portfolio quality
