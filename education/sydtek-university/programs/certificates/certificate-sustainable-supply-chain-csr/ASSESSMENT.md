# Assessment Framework

## University Track Grading

- Discussions and participation — 15%
- Case briefs and short memos — 20%
- Applied assignments — 25%
- Technology / compliance analysis — 15%
- Final capstone project — 25%

## Scholars Track Completion Model

- Module reflections — 20%
- Case study responses — 20%
- Project artifacts — 30%
- Final presentation or portfolio — 30%

## Signature Assessments

### 1. Supply Chain Mapping Artifact
Students diagram a product or service supply chain and identify:
- suppliers
- logistics nodes
- emissions touchpoints
- labor / ethical risk points
- resilience weak spots

### 2. CSR and Governance Memo
Students write a concise memo evaluating how an organization handles:
- labor standards
- ethical sourcing
- environmental reporting
- stakeholder communication
- public trust

### 3. Technology Readiness Brief
Students assess whether AI, blockchain, IoT, or digital twins would improve:
- traceability
- transparency
- planning
- carbon measurement
- supplier coordination

### 4. Final Capstone
Students create a practical sustainable supply chain transformation plan including:
- current-state diagnosis
- risk matrix
- recommended interventions
- implementation phases
- KPIs
- reporting and governance notes
