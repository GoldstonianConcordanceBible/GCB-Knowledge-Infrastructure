# Pinned Repo Blurb

The **Certificate in Sustainable Supply Chain Management & Corporate Social Responsibility** is a GitHub-native academic program scaffold for **SydTek University** and **SydTek Scholars**. It trains learners to understand and improve supply chains through sustainability, ethics, governance, resilience, transparency, and emerging technology.

This repository includes the full structure for course delivery, assignments, rubrics, portfolio artifacts, capstone strategy work, accreditation alignment, instructor guidance, and quality-control layers.

Built for the AI age. Built for visible evidence. Built for real systems.
