# Assignment 02 — Blockchain Traceability Brief

## Objective

Explain when blockchain is useful for traceability and when it is unnecessary or overstated.

## Prompt

Address:

- what blockchain can and cannot verify
- what still depends on real-world data integrity
- which organizations benefit most
- what governance issues remain even with blockchain
- when another system may be better

## Deliverable

- 900 to 1200 word brief
