# Case 01 — Food Traceability Blockchain Pilot

## Summary

A food distributor launches a blockchain traceability pilot to improve provenance claims for premium products. The system records shipment events and supplier entries, but internal review shows that several upstream data points are still manually entered without verification.

## Learning Purpose

This case helps students distinguish between:

- traceability claims
- data integrity
- governance design
- real value versus innovation theater

## Key Questions

1. What part of the system is actually stronger because of blockchain?
2. What still depends on trust outside the ledger?
3. Is the pilot solving the right problem?
4. What alternative or complementary controls are needed?
