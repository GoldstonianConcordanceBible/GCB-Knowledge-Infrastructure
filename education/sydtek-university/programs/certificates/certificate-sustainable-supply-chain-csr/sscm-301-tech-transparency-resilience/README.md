# SSCM 301 — Technology for Supply Chain Transparency and Resilience

## Course Overview

SSCM 301 explores the technologies increasingly used to strengthen supply chain visibility, traceability, forecasting, coordination, and resilience.

Students study AI, blockchain, IoT, analytics, and digital twins not as buzzwords, but as tools that may or may not solve actual operational problems depending on implementation quality.

The course teaches technological discernment alongside innovation.

---

## Course Description

This course examines how emerging technologies can support sustainable and resilient supply chain systems. Students analyze the promise and limits of AI-driven forecasting, blockchain-enabled traceability, IoT visibility systems, predictive analytics, and digital twin modeling.

Special attention is given to governance, data quality, system trust, and the danger of mistaking dashboards for truth.

---

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, learners will be able to:

1. Explain major technology categories used in supply chains.
2. Match technologies to real operational use cases.
3. Evaluate transparency and traceability systems.
4. Assess risks related to data quality and implementation failure.
5. Explain how digital tools can support sustainability goals.
6. Write a practical technology recommendation brief.

## Signature Assignments

### 1. Technology Comparison Matrix
Students compare AI, blockchain, IoT, and digital twins across:
- cost
- scalability
- transparency value
- operational fit
- sustainability value
- implementation risk

### 2. Blockchain Traceability Brief
Students explain when blockchain is helpful, when it is unnecessary, and what governance is still required.

### 3. Digital Twin Use-Case Analysis
Students describe how a digital twin could help an organization test supply chain redesign choices before implementation.

## Discussion Prompts
