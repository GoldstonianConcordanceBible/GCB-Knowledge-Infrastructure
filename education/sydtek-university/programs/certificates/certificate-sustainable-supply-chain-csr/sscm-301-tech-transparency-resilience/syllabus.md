# SSCM 301 Syllabus

## Course Title
SSCM 301 — Technology for Supply Chain Transparency and Resilience

## Course Length
8 weeks

## Credit Value
3 credit hours university track  
Scholars equivalent: Module 3

## Prerequisites
SSCM 101 recommended  
SSCM 201 recommended

## Assessment Breakdown
- discussions — 15%
- technology matrix — 20%
- blockchain brief — 20%
- digital twin analysis — 20%
- final recommendation brief — 25%

## Course Focus
This course emphasizes applied technological judgment rather than hype.

## Completion Standard
Students must demonstrate that they can evaluate technology choices in light of operational reality, sustainability, and governance.
