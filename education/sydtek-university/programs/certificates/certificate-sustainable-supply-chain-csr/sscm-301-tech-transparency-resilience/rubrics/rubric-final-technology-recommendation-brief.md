# Rubric — Final Technology Recommendation Brief

## Criteria

### 1. Problem Definition
The operational problem is clearly defined and realistically framed.

### 2. Technology Fit
The recommended tool or mix of tools aligns with the real need.

### 3. Governance Awareness
The brief addresses data quality, oversight, implementation risk, and organizational readiness.

### 4. Practicality
The recommendation is realistic in sequence, scope, and expected outcomes.

### 5. Communication
The writing is concise, structured, and persuasive.
