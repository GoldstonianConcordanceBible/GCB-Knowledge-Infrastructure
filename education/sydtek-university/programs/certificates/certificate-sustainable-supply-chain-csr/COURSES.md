# Courses

## SSCM 101 — Foundations of Sustainable Supply Chains

### Course Description
Introduces the principles of supply chain management through the lens of sustainability, resilience, and long-term value creation. Students examine sourcing, production, transportation, warehousing, customer fulfillment, and risk with special attention to environmental and social impacts.

### Topics
- what a supply chain is
- triple bottom line and beyond
- sustainability vs durability vs resilience
- stakeholder mapping
- sourcing and supplier ecosystems
- emissions across the value chain
- disruption, uncertainty, and continuity planning
- trust, visibility, and coordination

### Learning Outcomes
Students will be able to:
- define sustainable supply chain management
- map a basic end-to-end supply chain
- identify operational and ethical weak points
- distinguish cost optimization from sustainable optimization

### Major Assignments
- supply chain map
- disruption analysis memo
- sustainability maturity self-assessment

## SSCM 201 — Corporate Social Responsibility, Ethics, and Stakeholder Governance

### Course Description
Examines CSR as an operational and strategic framework rather than a branding exercise. Students study ethical sourcing, labor conditions, governance, anti-corruption, stakeholder trust, and the relationship between reputation and responsible operations.

### Topics
- history and evolution of CSR
- stakeholder theory
- labor, safety, and human rights in the supply chain
- anti-corruption and governance
- philanthropy vs embedded responsibility
- reputation risk and trust
- crisis-era CSR
- human capital as organizational capital

### Learning Outcomes
Students will be able to:
- explain the business case for CSR
- evaluate a firm’s CSR posture
- identify reputational and governance risks
- propose a stakeholder-centered improvement strategy

### Major Assignments
- CSR scorecard
- supplier ethics case brief
- board memo on stakeholder risk

## SSCM 301 — Technology for Supply Chain Transparency and Resilience

### Course Description
Focuses on the technologies reshaping supply chain management, including AI, analytics, blockchain, IoT, and digital twins. Students explore how visibility and traceability tools strengthen sustainability, resilience, and compliance.

### Topics
- AI in forecasting and planning
- blockchain for traceability
- IoT and end-to-end visibility
- digital twins
- predictive analytics
- supplier transparency dashboards
- carbon tracking and measurement systems
- technology limitations and governance

### Learning Outcomes
Students will be able to:
- explain core technology use cases in supply chains
- evaluate which tools fit which operational problems
- connect transparency systems to sustainability goals
- assess basic risks of overreliance on technology

### Major Assignments
- technology comparison matrix
- blockchain traceability brief
- digital twin use-case analysis

## SSCM 401 — Capstone: Sustainable Supply Chain Strategy Lab

### Course Description
A hands-on capstone in which students diagnose a real or hypothetical organization’s supply chain and build a sustainable improvement strategy. Students integrate operations, ethics, stakeholder governance, technology, and reporting readiness.

### Topics
- supply chain diagnosis
- supplier risk prioritization
- ESG / climate disclosure basics
- change management
- collaboration and implementation planning
- KPI design
- executive communication
- final portfolio build

### Learning Outcomes
Students will be able to:
- produce a complete sustainable supply chain strategy
- prioritize interventions across cost, ethics, and resilience
- communicate recommendations to executives and stakeholders
- defend a transformation roadmap with evidence

### Major Assignments
- capstone proposal
- current-state risk map
- sustainable transformation roadmap
- executive presentation
- final GitHub portfolio submission
