# G-Work Trade School

## SydTek University

G-Work Trade School is the workforce development, certification, and marketplace infrastructure layer for AI-proof skilled labor.

This initiative sits inside SydTek University as a practical trade-school system that connects:

- Training
- Verification
- Apprenticeship
- Real-world jobs
- Digital Union certification
- Local skilled labor deployment

G-Work is not a gig-work platform.

G-Work is the infrastructure layer for skilled trades.

## Core Thesis

Artificial intelligence will automate many digital and administrative tasks, but it cannot replace real-world physical execution at scale.

Plumbing, electrical work, HVAC, auto repair, installation, maintenance, and field-based IT require trust, skill, presence, and accountability.

G-Work exists to organize that labor into a verified, trusted, high-standard network.

## Operating Doctrine

Fluency, not exposure.

Students and workers do not merely learn about trades.

They practice, verify, work, get rated, improve, certify, and eventually teach.

## Core Layers

1. SydTek University  
   The education and training layer.

2. G-Work  
   The marketplace and job-matching layer.

3. The Digital Union  
   The certification, vetting, and quality-standard layer.

4. Local Pilot Markets  
   The city-by-city deployment layer.

## Initial Focus Areas

- Electrical
- Plumbing
- HVAC
- Auto repair
- Field IT
- Virtual IT support
- General maintenance
- Beauty and service professionals as an early adjacent pilot

## Day Zero Philosophy

Day Zero is not a public launch.

Day Zero is the controlled ignition of the network inside SydTek University.

The goal is to prove the trust loop before scaling:

Worker verified → job assigned → work completed → customer reviews → worker earns status → customer returns.

## Repository Purpose

This repository documents the operating system for launching G-Work through SydTek University, beginning with Day Zero and expanding into verified trade-school infrastructure.
