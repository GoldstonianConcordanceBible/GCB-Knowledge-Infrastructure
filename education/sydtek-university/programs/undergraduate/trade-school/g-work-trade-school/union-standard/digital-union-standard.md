# The Digital Union Standard

## Purpose

The Digital Union is the certification and quality-standard layer for G-Work.

G-Work provides access to jobs.

The Digital Union defines who is trusted to receive them.

## Core Positioning

The Digital Union is not a traditional labor union.

It is a digital certification authority for skilled, verified, AI-proof labor.

Its purpose is to establish a high-trust standard for tradespeople, service professionals, and field workers.

## Standard Thesis

In skilled trades, trust is the product.

Customers are not only buying labor.

They are buying safety, reliability, workmanship, and accountability.

## Member Philosophy

Workers are not simply onboarded.

They are admitted into a standard.

## Four-Part Vetting System

### 1. Legacy Check

The Legacy Check reviews the worker's professional standing.

Criteria may include:

- License status
- Board standing
- Complaint history
- Years of experience
- Business history
- Reference checks

### 2. Peer Review Loop

The Peer Review Loop allows trusted members to validate new members.

This creates network-enforced trust.

Criteria may include:

- Sponsorship by an existing member
- Verified references
- Peer endorsement
- Field reputation

### 3. Insurance Vault

The Insurance Vault tracks proof of active insurance.

The long-term goal is live verification, not static PDF storage.

Criteria may include:

- Insurance certificate upload
- Expiration date tracking
- Coverage category
- Renewal reminders

### 4. Professional Tech Stack

Digital Union members must use standardized tools for:

- Invoicing
- Job tracking
- Customer communication
- Proof-of-work documentation
- Ratings and reviews

The customer experience should feel consistent, premium, and reliable.

## Membership Tiers

### Applicant

The worker has applied but is not yet visible to customers.

### Verified

The worker has passed basic identity, skill, and reliability review.

### Certified

The worker has completed jobs successfully and has strong customer feedback.

### Elite

The worker represents the highest standard of the network.

Elite members may mentor, train, sponsor, or instruct through SydTek University.

## Badge

The official badge is:

Digital Union Certified

This badge may appear on:

- Worker profiles
- Invoices
- Trucks
- Uniforms
- Marketing material
- Certificates
- Training documents

## Core Rule

The badge must mean something.

If the standard is weak, the brand collapses.

If the standard is strong, the badge becomes the moat.
