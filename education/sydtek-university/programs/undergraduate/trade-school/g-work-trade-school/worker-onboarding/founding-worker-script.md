# Founding Worker Script

## Purpose

This script is used to recruit the first G-Work workers for the Day Zero pilot.

## Message

We are forming the founding class of G-Work, a private skilled-labor network connected to SydTek University.

This is not a generic gig app.

The goal is to build a verified network of reliable tradespeople and service professionals who can receive trusted job opportunities through a high-standard system.

We are beginning with a controlled pilot.

Founding members will receive:

- Early access to job opportunities
- Zero commission during the pilot period
- Priority consideration for future platform visibility
- Early Digital Union verification status
- Opportunity to help shape the standard

We are looking for people who are:

- Reliable
- Skilled
- Professional
- Responsive
- Trustworthy
- Serious about their craft

This first group will help define what quality looks like inside the G-Work network.

If selected, you will complete a short intake process and may be matched with early pilot jobs.
