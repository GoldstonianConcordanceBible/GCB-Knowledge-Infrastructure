## Credit Philosophy

Credit represents verified learning, not passive time.

## Credit May Be Awarded For

- Direct instruction
- Guided practice
- Fieldwork
- Research
- Portfolio production
- Demonstrated competency
- Final assessment

## Credit May Not Be Awarded For

- Attendance alone
- Payment alone
- Passive video watching
- Unverified claims
- Incomplete work

## Credit Evidence

Each credit must be supported by:

- Learning activity
- Artifact or log
- Assessment record
- Instructor approval

## Core Principle

Credit follows documented mastery.
