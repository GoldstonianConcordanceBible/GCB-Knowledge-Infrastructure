# Certificate in Sustainable Supply Chain Management & Corporate Social Responsibility
## SydTek University × SydTek Scholars

The **Certificate in Sustainable Supply Chain Management & Corporate Social Responsibility** prepares learners to understand, evaluate, and improve modern supply chains through the lenses of sustainability, ethics, resilience, transparency, and emerging technology.

This program is designed for the AI age.

It recognizes that modern organizations are no longer judged only by cost, speed, and output.
They are also judged by:

- how they treat people
- how they manage suppliers
- how transparent they are
- how they respond to disruption
- how they use technology
- how they address environmental and governance risk
- how well they maintain stakeholder trust

This certificate gives SydTek University and SydTek Scholars a practical, GitHub-ready pathway into one of the most important operational disciplines of the modern economy.

---

## Program Vision

This certificate exists to train learners who can think beyond logistics in the narrow sense.

Students in this program learn to see supply chains as:

- systems of coordination
- systems of accountability
- systems of risk
- systems of stewardship
- systems of digital visibility
- systems of strategic advantage

The goal is not simply to create better operators.
The goal is to create leaders who can make organizations more resilient, ethical, and sustainable.

## Program Learning Outcomes

By the end of the certificate, learners will be able to:

1. Explain the relationship between sustainability, CSR, and supply chain strategy.
2. Analyze operational, ethical, environmental, and governance risks across a supply chain.
3. Map supplier, logistics, and stakeholder relationships.
4. Evaluate transparency and traceability challenges.
5. Assess the use of AI, blockchain, analytics, IoT, and digital twins in supply chain systems.
6. Interpret basic ESG, disclosure, and stakeholder expectations.
7. Design practical improvement strategies for resilient and responsible operations.
8. Communicate findings in portfolio-ready, executive-friendly formats.

---

## Program Structure

### SSCM 101 — Foundations of Sustainable Supply Chains
Introduces supply chains as systems of flow, risk, sustainability, and resilience.

### SSCM 201 — Corporate Social Responsibility, Ethics, and Stakeholder Governance
Examines responsibility as an operational system tied to trust, labor, sourcing, and governance.

### SSCM 301 — Technology for Supply Chain Transparency and Resilience
Explores AI, blockchain, IoT, analytics, and digital twins as tools for visibility, resilience, and strategic decision-making.

### SSCM 401 — Capstone: Sustainable Supply Chain Strategy Lab
Requires students to produce a practical transformation strategy for a real or hypothetical organization.
