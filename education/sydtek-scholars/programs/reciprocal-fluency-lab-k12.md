# SydTek Scholars K–12 Reciprocal Fluency Lab

## Purpose

This program adapts the SydTek University Reciprocal Fluency Lab for K–12 homeschool and private online learners.

## Program Name

SydTek Scholars Reciprocal Fluency Lab

## Credential Type

K–12 mastery badge / homeschool portfolio credential

## Parent Program

SydTek University Certificate in Reciprocal Fluency Lab

## Doctrine

Fluency, not exposure.

## Method

Mirror → Water → Fire

## Program Purpose

To help K–12 students develop real bilingual communication through guided peer practice, parent oversight, instructor review, and age-appropriate assessment.

## Student Audience

- Homeschool students
- Private online school students
- Family learning pods
- Early Spanish learners
- Bilingual household learners

## Safety Rule

K–12 students must participate with parent, guardian, or approved instructor oversight.

## Pairing Model

Students may be paired through:

- Family-to-family exchange
- Instructor-led pods
- Parent-supervised sessions
- Approved student groups

## Learning Evidence

Students complete:

- Speaking samples
- Listening practice
- Vocabulary logs
- Parent verification
- Instructor review
- Portfolio reflections
- Age-appropriate oral demonstrations

## Levels

### Level A — First Words

Student can say basic words and phrases.

### Level B — Simple Sentences

Student can answer basic questions.

### Level C — Guided Conversation

Student can participate in short conversations.

### Level D — Confident Exchange

Student can speak, listen, and respond with growing independence.

## Assessment

Assessment may include:

- Parent observation
- Instructor listening review
- Short oral check
- Recorded reading
- Conversation sample
- Portfolio review

## Core Principle

Children prove language by speaking it, hearing it, and using it with people.
