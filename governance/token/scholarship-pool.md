# $GCB Scholarship Pool

## Purpose

Defines how $GCB may support learner access without weakening certification standards.

---

## Scholarship Pool Function

The scholarship pool may help cover:

- Program access
- Assessment fees
- Learning materials
- Mentor support
- Platform costs

---

## Eligibility

Students may qualify based on:

- Financial need
- Service commitment
- Ministry work
- Workforce development goals
- Family education participation
- Instructor recommendation

---

## Scholarship Limits

Scholarships do not guarantee certification.

Scholarships provide access to the pathway.

---

## Required Standard

Scholarship recipients must still complete:

- Sessions
- Logs
- Partner verification
- Instructor review
- Final assessment

---

## Anti-Abuse Controls

Scholarships may be paused if:

- Student stops participating
- Logs are falsified
- Conduct rules are violated
- Assessment is avoided

---

## Core Principle

Scholarship opens the door.

Fluency earns the certificate.
