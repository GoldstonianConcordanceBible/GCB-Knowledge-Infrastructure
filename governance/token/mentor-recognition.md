# Mentor Recognition Policy

## Purpose

Defines how mentors may be recognized through reputation, status, and possible token-based recognition.

---

## Mentor Role

Mentors help new learners succeed.

They support:

- Conversation practice
- Correction
- Encouragement
- Session discipline
- Cultural respect

---

## Recognition Types

Mentors may receive:

- Mentor badge
- Reputation increase
- Governance eligibility
- Advanced program access
- Limited $GCB recognition

---

## Eligibility

Mentors must:

- Hold a valid certificate
- Maintain reputation score ≥ 4
- Have instructor approval
- Demonstrate consistent service

---

## Token Recognition

Token-based recognition may be issued only after verified service.

Examples:

- Helping a learner complete certification
- Leading approved practice pods
- Supporting curriculum translation
- Completing instructor-approved service hours

---

## Core Principle

Mentors are rewarded for multiplying fluency, not collecting attendance.
