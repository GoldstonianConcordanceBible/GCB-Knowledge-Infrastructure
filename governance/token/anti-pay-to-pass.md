# Anti Pay-To-Pass Policy

## Purpose

Protects SydTek University certificates from becoming purchasable credentials.

---

## Core Rule

No person may buy certification.

---

## Payment May Cover

Payments or tokens may cover:

- Program access
- Instructor time
- Platform costs
- Materials
- Assessment administration

---

## Payment May Not Cover

Payments or tokens may not cover:

- Passing status
- Higher fluency level
- Instructor approval
- Rubric score
- Assessment exemption
- Certificate issuance without proof

---

## Enforcement

Any suspected pay-to-pass activity triggers review.

Consequences may include:

- Certificate denial
- Certificate revocation
- Instructor removal
- Governance review

---

## Core Principle

Access can be purchased.

Achievement must be proven.
