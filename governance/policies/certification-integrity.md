# Certification Integrity Policy

## Purpose

Protects the credibility of all SydTek University certificates.

---

## Core Rule

No certificate is issued without proof.

---

## Required Evidence

Certification must include:

- Verified session logs
- Partner validation
- Instructor review
- Recorded speaking samples
- Final oral assessment

---

## Prohibited Actions

- Issuing certificates without assessment
- Accepting fake logs
- Skipping final evaluation
- Granting certification based on time alone

---

## Audit System

Random audits may include:

- Reviewing recordings
- Re-testing students
- Checking session authenticity

---

## Revocation

Certificates may be revoked if:

- Fraud is discovered
- Logs are falsified
- Assessment integrity is compromised

---

## Core Principle

A weak certificate destroys the system.
