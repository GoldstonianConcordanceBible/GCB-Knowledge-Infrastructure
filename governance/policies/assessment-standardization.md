# Assessment Standardization Policy

## Purpose

Ensures all assessments are consistent across the system.

---

## Standard Components

All assessments must evaluate:

- Speaking
- Listening
- Pronunciation
- Response speed
- Correction application

---

## Format

- Live oral assessment required
- Instructor-led evaluation
- Structured rubric scoring

---

## Calibration

Instructors must:

- Review sample assessments
- Align scoring standards
- Participate in periodic calibration

---

## Consistency Rule

A Level 2 certificate must mean the same across all students.

---

## Core Principle

Standardization protects credibility.
