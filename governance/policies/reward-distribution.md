# Reward Distribution Policy

## Purpose

Defines how rewards are distributed fairly and securely.

---

## Core Rule

Distribution follows verified contribution.

---

## Distribution Process

1. Event occurs (certification, mentorship, contribution)
2. Evidence verified
3. Instructor or council approval
4. Reward issued
5. Logged in system

---

## Controls

- No automatic payouts
- No anonymous rewards
- No unverified distribution
- All rewards logged

---

## Frequency

Rewards may be:

- Periodic
- Milestone-based
- Contribution-based

---

## Transparency

All reward events must be:

- Documented
- Traceable
- Reviewable

---

## Core Principle

Every reward must be explainable.
