# Authority Separation Policy

## Purpose

Prevents collapse of governance by clearly separating roles across layers.

---

## Three Authority Types

### 1. Doctrine Authority (GCB)

Defines truth, framework, and canonical structure.

### 2. Governance Authority (SydTek University)

Defines programs, operations, and certification.

### 3. Delivery Authority (SydTek Scholars)

Implements learning and engages students.

---

## Critical Rule

No layer may assume the authority of another layer.

---

## Violations to Avoid

### Governance Overreach

SydTek University attempting to redefine doctrine.

### Delivery Overreach

SydTek Scholars attempting to issue independent certifications.

### Doctrine Drift

Changing foundational principles due to operational pressure.

---

## Protection Mechanisms

- Documentation lock-in (GitHub)
- Clear governance charters
- Instructor oversight
- Certification standards enforcement

---

## Failure Mode

If layers collapse:

- Doctrine becomes unstable
- Certificates lose credibility
- System becomes chaotic

---

## Core Principle

Separation creates strength.
