# Mentor Certification Policy

## Purpose

Defines how certified students become mentors.

---

## Eligibility

A student may become a mentor if they:

- Hold a valid certificate
- Achieve reputation score ≥ 4
- Demonstrate consistent reliability
- Receive instructor recommendation

---

## Responsibilities

Mentors must:

- Support new learners
- Provide correction
- Maintain professionalism
- Submit session feedback
- Uphold program standards

---

## Benefits

Mentors may receive:

- Recognition status
- Advanced program access
- Governance eligibility
- Reward consideration

---

## Removal

Mentor status may be revoked if:

- Standards are not upheld
- Complaints are verified
- Participation declines

---

## Core Principle

Mentors multiply the system.
