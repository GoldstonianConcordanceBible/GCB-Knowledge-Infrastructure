# Data Integrity Policy

## Purpose

Ensures accuracy and trust in all recorded program data.

---

## Data Types

- Session logs
- Assessment scores
- Certificates
- Reputation scores
- Reward records

---

## Requirements

All data must be:

- Verifiable
- Consistent
- Traceable
- Protected

---

## Prohibited Actions

- Editing logs after submission
- Creating fake entries
- Manipulating scores
- Deleting audit records

---

## Audit Access

Authorized roles may review data for:

- Verification
- Investigation
- Quality control

---

## Core Principle

If data cannot be trusted, the system fails.
