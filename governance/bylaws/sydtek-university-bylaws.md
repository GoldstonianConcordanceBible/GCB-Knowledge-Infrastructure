# SydTek University DAO Bylaws

## Purpose

These bylaws define the operational governance rules for SydTek University as an institutional DAO.

---

## Article I — Authority

SydTek University operates as the execution layer of the GCB ecosystem.

It holds authority over:

- Programs
- Certificates
- Curriculum
- Operations
- Institutional partnerships

It does not hold authority over doctrine.

---

## Article II — Governance Bodies

### 1. Program Director

- Final decision authority
- Approves certifications and major changes
- Can override votes in emergencies

### 2. Instructor Council

- Maintains assessment standards
- Reviews certification integrity
- Conducts audits

### 3. Mentor Council

- Represents certified participants
- Supports scaling and peer learning
- Advises on program quality

### 4. Contributors

- Verified participants eligible to vote
- Must meet reputation and certification requirements

---

## Article III — Voting

### Eligibility

Participants must:

- Hold a valid certificate
- Maintain a reputation score ≥ 3
- Have no active violations

### Voting Scope

Eligible voters may vote on:

- Program updates
- Curriculum improvements
- Reward policies
- Expansion initiatives

### Voting Restrictions

No vote may:

- Remove final assessments
- Lower certification standards
- Override GCB doctrine

---

## Article IV — Certification Authority

Certification requires:

- Completed sessions
- Verified logs
- Instructor review
- Final oral assessment

No automated certification is allowed.

---

## Article V — Enforcement

The DAO may:

- Pause certification
- Remove participants
- Revoke rewards
- Require reassessment

---

## Article VI — Amendments

Bylaws may be updated only if:

- Approved by Program Director
- Supported by Instructor Council
- Documented in GitHub

---

## Core Principle

Governance must protect the value of certification.
