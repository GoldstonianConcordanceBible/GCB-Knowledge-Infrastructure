# SydTek Scholars Sub-DAO Bylaws

## Purpose

Defines governance rules for SydTek Scholars as a sub-DAO.

---

## Article I — Role

SydTek Scholars delivers foundational education.

It supports:

- K–12 learning
- Homeschool families
- Early language programs

---

## Article II — Authority

SydTek Scholars may:

- Manage curriculum delivery
- Organize learning groups
- Support student engagement
- Facilitate early-stage programs

SydTek Scholars may not:

- Issue independent certificates
- Modify institutional assessment standards
- Override SydTek University governance

---

## Article III — Governance Bodies

### Program Leads

- Oversee curriculum execution

### Family Coordinators

- Support parents and learners

### Mentor Students

- Assist beginner learners

---

## Article IV — Participation

Participants include:

- Students
- Parents
- Mentors
- Coordinators

---

## Article V — Alignment

All programs must align with:

- SydTek University standards
- GCB doctrine

---

## Article VI — Enforcement

Violations may result in:

- Removal from program
- Reassignment
- Review by SydTek University

---

## Core Principle

Delivery must remain aligned with institutional standards.
