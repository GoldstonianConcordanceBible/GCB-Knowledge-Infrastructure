# Certificate Revocation Policy

## Purpose

Defines how SydTek University may revoke a certificate while preserving transparency.

---

## Grounds for Revocation

A certificate may be revoked if:

- Session logs were falsified
- Assessment fraud occurred
- Identity fraud occurred
- Instructor approval was manipulated
- Serious conduct violations are discovered

---

## Revocation Authority

Only SydTek University may revoke a SydTek University certificate.

Recommended approval:

- Program Director
- Instructor Council review
- Written evidence file

---

## Revocation Record

A revocation record should include:

- Certificate ID
- Revocation date
- Status change
- General reason category
- Revoking authority

Do not include private details publicly.

---

## Appeals

A student may request review.

Appeals may include:

- Reassessment
- Evidence review
- Instructor panel decision

---

## Core Principle

Revocation protects the value of honest certificates.
