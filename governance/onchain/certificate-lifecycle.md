# Certificate Lifecycle

## Purpose

Defines the lifecycle of a SydTek University certificate from assessment to verification.

## Lifecycle Stages

### 1. Candidate

Student is enrolled but not yet certified.

### 2. Assessment Pending

Student has completed required sessions and awaits final assessment.

### 3. Assessed

Instructor has completed evaluation.

### 4. Approved

Program authority approves certificate issuance.

### 5. Issued

Certificate record is issued.

### 6. Verified

Credential is publicly verifiable.

### 7. Renewed

Credential is updated after continued demonstration or reassessment.

### 8. Revoked

Credential is invalidated due to fraud or serious violation.

### 9. Replaced

Credential is reissued due to wallet recovery or administrative correction.

## Core Principle

Certification is a lifecycle, not a single PDF.
