# Soulbound Credential Policy

## Purpose

Defines the use of non-transferable credentials for SydTek University certificates.

---

## Why Non-Transferable

A fluency certificate represents personal ability.

It should not be sold, traded, or transferred.

---

## Credential Type

Certificates should be issued as:

- Non-transferable NFT
- Soulbound token
- Verifiable credential
- Wallet-bound credential record

---

## Transfer Rule

The credential must not be transferable between wallets without institutional review.

---

## Wallet Recovery

If a student loses access to a wallet, SydTek University may reissue the credential after identity verification.

---

## Public Metadata

Public metadata may include:

- Program name
- Certification level
- Issue date
- Credential status
- Institution

---

## Private Metadata

Private metadata should remain off-chain.

---

## Core Principle

A certificate belongs to the learner’s demonstrated ability, not the market.
