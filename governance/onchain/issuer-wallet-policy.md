# Issuer Wallet Policy

## Purpose

Defines how SydTek University manages wallets used for certificate issuance.

## Core Rule

Only approved institutional wallets may issue certificate records.

## Wallet Types

### Institutional Wallet

Represents SydTek University.

### Program Wallet

Represents a specific certificate program.

### Issuer Wallet

Used by approved certifiers or authorized staff.

## Approval

Issuer wallets must be approved by:

- Program Director
- Instructor Council
- Governance record

## Security Requirements

Issuer wallets must use:

- Strong custody controls
- Limited permissions
- Documented approval
- Revocation ability

## Prohibited Use

Issuer wallets may not be used for:

- Personal trading
- Unauthorized rewards
- Private payments
- Unapproved certificate issuance

## Core Principle

The issuer wallet represents institutional trust.
