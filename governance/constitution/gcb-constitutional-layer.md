# GCB Constitutional Layer

## Purpose

This document defines the role of the Goldstonian Concordance Bible (GCB) as the constitutional foundation of the ecosystem.

---

## Definition

The GCB is the governing doctrinal framework that establishes:

- The Mirror → Water → Fire Doctrine
- Canonical interpretation standards
- Theological consistency
- Knowledge graph structure
- GEO alignment principles

---

## Function

The GCB acts as:

- A constitutional reference
- A theological anchor
- A non-volatile knowledge system

---

## Governance Model

The GCB is not governed like a typical DAO.

It may include:

- Advisory council input
- Scholarly review
- Controlled updates

It does not include:

- Frequent voting
- Rapid policy changes
- Open governance participation

---

## Protection Rule

No downstream DAO may override or redefine:

- Mirror → Water → Fire Doctrine
- Canonical structure
- Core theological positions

---

## Update Mechanism

Changes to GCB must be:

- Rare
- Documented
- Justified
- Reviewed at the highest level

---

## Role in Ecosystem

GCB provides:

- Direction
- Stability
- Authority

---

## Core Principle

A system without a fixed foundation cannot scale.

GCB is the foundation.
