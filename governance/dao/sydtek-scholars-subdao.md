# SydTek Scholars Sub-DAO

## Purpose

Defines the role of SydTek Scholars as a sub-DAO under SydTek University.

---

## Role

SydTek Scholars delivers education at the foundational level.

It focuses on:

- K–12 learners
- Homeschool systems
- Family-based education
- Early language development
- Foundational curriculum

---

## Authority Scope

SydTek Scholars governs:

- Curriculum delivery methods
- Student engagement systems
- Family learning models
- Early-stage programs
- Language foundations (e.g., Spanish fluency tracks)

---

## Relationship to SydTek University

SydTek Scholars operates under SydTek University and must align with:

- Institutional standards
- Assessment frameworks
- Certification pathways

---

## Governance Structure

### Program Leads

Oversee curriculum delivery.

### Family Coordinators

Support homeschool and parent-led learning.

### Mentor Students

Advanced learners who support beginners.

---

## Governance Limits

SydTek Scholars may not:

- Issue independent certificates outside SydTek University
- Modify institutional assessment standards
- Override doctrine or program requirements

---

## Strategic Role

SydTek Scholars acts as:

- The entry point into the ecosystem
- The pipeline into SydTek University programs
- The foundation for long-term learner development

---

## Core Principle

Strong foundations create strong outcomes.
