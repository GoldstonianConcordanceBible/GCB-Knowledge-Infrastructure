# SydTek University DAO

## Purpose

Defines governance, authority, and responsibilities of SydTek University as the institutional DAO.

---

## Role

SydTek University is the execution layer of the GCB ecosystem.

It transforms doctrine into:

- Programs
- Certificates
- Curriculum
- Institutional systems

---

## Authority Scope

SydTek University governs:

- Program creation
- Certificate approval
- Instructor certification
- Curriculum updates
- Operational policy
- Token integration (if applicable)
- Partnerships and expansion

---

## DAO Structure

### Program Director

- Final authority on academic and program decisions

### Instructor Council

- Maintains assessment standards
- Reviews certification quality

### Mentor Council

- Represents certified students
- Supports scaling and peer learning

### Contributors

- Verified participants with governance eligibility

---

## Governance Actions

The DAO may:

- Approve new certificate programs
- Modify learning structures
- Adjust assessment frameworks
- Approve reward policies
- Expand into new domains

---

## Governance Limits

The DAO may not:

- Override GCB doctrine
- Remove assessment requirements
- Weaken certification standards

---

## Voting Eligibility

Participants must:

- Hold a valid certificate
- Maintain strong reputation score
- Have no active violations
- Demonstrate contribution

---

## Core Principle

Governance exists to scale quality, not reduce it.
