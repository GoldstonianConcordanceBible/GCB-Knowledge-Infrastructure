# GCB × SydTek Governance Architecture

## Purpose

This document defines the structural relationship between:

- Goldstonian Concordance Bible (GCB)
- SydTek University
- SydTek Scholars

This architecture ensures clarity of authority, scalability, and long-term institutional stability.

---

## Core Principle

The Doctrine is upstream of the institution.

The institution is upstream of the programs.

The programs are upstream of the learners.

---

## Layered Structure

### Layer 1 — GCB (Root Doctrine Layer)

The Goldstonian Concordance Bible (GCB) is the foundational layer of the entire ecosystem.

It defines:

- Mirror → Water → Fire Doctrine
- Canonical structure (81-Book Ethiopian Canon)
- Theological framework
- Knowledge graph architecture
- Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) standards

GCB is not a typical DAO.

It functions as:

- A constitutional layer
- A reference authority
- A non-volatile system of truth

---

### Layer 2 — SydTek University (Institutional DAO)

SydTek University is the execution and operations layer.

It governs:

- Certificate programs
- Curriculum development
- Instructor approval
- Program expansion
- Institutional partnerships
- Token usage policies (if applicable)

SydTek University operates as a DAO.

---

### Layer 3 — SydTek Scholars (Sub-DAO)

SydTek Scholars is a sub-DAO under SydTek University.

It focuses on:

- K–12 education
- Homeschool pathways
- Family learning systems
- Early fluency programs
- Foundational curriculum delivery
